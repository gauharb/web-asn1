Steve Jobs

1955 - 2011

About Steve Jobs

Steve Jobs was an American businessman and one of the co-founders of Apple. He was born in 1955 and died in 2011. He was involved in products such as the Macintosh, iPod, iPhone and iPad. His work had a strong influence on personal technology and product design.

Important events

  1. 1955 - Steve Jobs was born in San Francisco, California.
  2. 1976 - Apple was founded by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne.
  3. 1984 - Apple introduced the Macintosh computer.
  4. 1997 - Jobs returned to Apple.
  5. 2007 - Apple introduced the first iPhone.
  6. 2011 - Steve Jobs died at the age of 56.

Key contributions

  • Helped develop and promote the Macintosh.
  • Was involved in the iPod and iTunes products.
  • Helped introduce the iPhone to the market.
  • Focused strongly on product design and user experience.
